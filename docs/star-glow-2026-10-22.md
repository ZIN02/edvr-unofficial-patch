# Star glow: a stronger bloom halo around stars, by star (2026-10-22)

## Status

- **State: DESIGN, NOT BUILT.** A user asked for the old double bloom back,
  for stars only. That was the 2026-10-20 bug: the HUD re-issue added
  T(bloom) over the frame, which doubled every halo (cockpit-hud-layer
  doc). Sean's shape:
  - a setting of 100, 125, 150, 175 or 200%, where 100 is stock and runs
    nothing;
  - then the strength per star from the star's own class.
- **Mechanism (planned):** at the bloom chain's first level, the HUD bloom
  feed's pass binds H' = H + HUD + sum over stars of (k_star - 1)·S_star.
  S_star is that star's draws replayed against the final depth, so the
  game's own bloom makes a stronger halo. Flat only; temporal AA on.
- **Engine survey FLOWN 2026-10-22** (temporary dump `6454231b`, log
  `edvr_gfx_20261022_080341`). Five stars: K2 V, M2 V, G, DC9 white dwarf,
  B (Alkaid). Findings:
  - **Radius, exact:** VS b2 [0].w of the halo-ring (5E417E9D) or arc
    (1F3AD158) draw. It matches the journal's Radius bit for bit (M2
    280954976, WD 14793806). From VS b0 alone, R = sqrt(d·(d - |t_ring|))
    reproduces K2 and M2 exactly and is good at all five stars.
  - **Distance, exact:** VS b0 rows 9-11 .w of the sphere draw
    (0EE43D81/4D516EF0) is the camera-relative star centre, in metres.
  - **Colour:** PS b2 [0].yzw of D1281DF4/97DBC87F is peak-normalised RGB
    times S. The surface draw's PS b2 [0].xyz agrees. Blue/red is M2 0.06,
    B 3.2, WD 4.9. Calibration still needed.
  - **Not carried:** temperature as a number (a 15680 that looked like one
    is an exposure scalar) and a clean intrinsic intensity.
  - **Class:** white dwarf versus main sequence follows from radius (and a
    distinct parameter preset). OBAFGKM needs colour, which only 3 of 5
    stars gave.
- **Gap:** PS/VS b2 is mostly unshadowed (`untracked-no-private-copy`).
  The 64-entry tracked buffer table churns, so K2 and G gave no colour. b1
  was dumped only to 1024 of its 5376 bytes.
- **Recommended source (hybrid):** the journal for class, temperature,
  luminosity and radius per scanned star; the engine's exact radius per
  drawn star to say which drawn star is which body. That solves the
  multi-star mapping. Colour stands in for an unscanned star, and failing
  that, stock.
- **Next:** decide hybrid versus engine-only. If engine-only, a second
  survey with b2 made readable on the star draws, near and far on one star,
  and scanned A, F, O, neutron and giant stars. Waiting for the honk gives
  each census its truth line.
- **Temporary instrument:** the flat star dump and the `journal star:` lines
  (`6454231b`). The latter put system and body names in logs. Remove both
  once the survey questions are closed.

At arrival, distance and radius are confounded for the main sequence
(d = 1.819·R + 6.7e7 m), so S cannot be separated from distance in this
survey. A Naka-Rushton fit of S on σT⁴(R/d)², trained on M2 and WD,
predicts 708 for B against 509 measured: that model is ruled out.

- ruled out: P1 PS b2 [2].z as a temperature, because it equals 2√2 × b1
  [62].y at both stars, an exposure scalar.
- ruled out: the 8BD7 instance count and the 1F3A index total as class
  signals, because neither is monotone in temperature or radius.

The full tables and the decoded buffer layouts are reproduced from the
survey analysis. They are not kept in the repository; the numbers above are
the ones the design rests on.

## The setting (proposed)

`fix.star_glow`, with choices 100, 125, 150, 175 and 200 (100 the default,
stock), plus a second choice for how it applies: the same for every star,
or by star class. Live; on F8's flat page and the installer. In the
by-class mode the setting is the peak: k = 1 + (peak - 1)·w(star).

| w | Classes |
|---|---|
| 1.0 | O, Wolf-Rayet, neutron |
| 0.85 | B |
| 0.75 | white dwarf |
| 0.65 | A, Herbig Ae/Be |
| 0.45 | F |
| 0.3 | G |
| 0.2 | K, T Tauri |
| 0.1-0.15 | M, carbon |
| 0 | brown dwarfs, black holes |

Luminosity classes I-III add 0.15. An unknown star stays at stock.
